# Changelog

## [v1.2] — 2026-11-10

- Keep the fish's color consistent as it settles onto the porcelain dish (1:17–1:18). It now takes on the dish's cobalt texture on contact, without overlapping fades.
- Let the code fish's probe sweep back and forth along the visible spine, appearing gradually as the fish enters the screen (2:14).
- Place live readings beside what they measure: heading and speed at the head, curvature at midbody, and wave displacement at the tail.

## [v1.1] — 2026-11-10

- Rework the oracle bone with a more organic outline, weathered surface, irregular cracks, and tapered knife cuts.
- Loosen the bronze fish's turn as it leaves the basin (0:46), and ease its slowdown onto the porcelain dish (1:17–1:18).
- Remove the vertical shadow seam between stations, most visible before the New Year print (1:40).
- Align the print transformation with the paper's edge. Keep the red frame behind the fish and stop frame borders and wall lines from showing through its body (1:41).
- Refine the paper-cut fish's scales, fins, and tail, keeping the silhouette connected through tight bends.
- Keep the two companion fish swimming in the Song painting during the finale, and remove the bright fin seam from the fish-shaped lighting (2:23).
- Add live motion readings and a moving probe to the code fish.
- Align the flute entrance with the clapper beat (0:54–0:55), and slightly raise the ambience, low drone, and keyboard sounds before the finale.
- Refresh the cover and station montage.

## [v1.0] — 2026-11-10

- Release the 2:34 film: eleven works of Chinese art, drawn in Canvas 2D with a synthesized Python score.
- Include the browser player, chapter navigation, frame stepping, still export, and parallel video rendering up to 4K at 60 fps.
- Publish the live experience and a 1080p, 30 fps film download with sound.
- Add English and Chinese documentation, the MIT license, and the embedded fonts' original licenses.
