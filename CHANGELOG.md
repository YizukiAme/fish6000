## [v1.5] — 2026-11-16

- Add brief holds after the oracle-bone crack and when the three fish meet in the bronze basin, while smoke and the twin fish's reactions continue.
- Redraw the Han fisherman with a kneeling silhouette, a bamboo hat, and hands that follow the lifting pole.
- Stagger the bronze twins' head turns, with a gentle tail response and settling motion.
- Redraw the game fish for each era: monochrome, four-tone green, 16-color pixel art, and shaded sprites. Keep their backs upright through turns and refine eyes, fins, and smaller fish.
- Add a detailed CRT housing, console, and wired controller to the pixel station.
- Soften the xun's breath texture and extend sustained sounds across the new pauses.
- Extend the film to 2:53.35 and update chapter timings and documentation.

## [v1.4] — 2026-11-16

- Give the pixel station four distinct games: a dot maze, scrolling coin collection, a fishhook escape, and chasing smaller fish (2:01–2:19).
- Add scores, lives, floating point awards, level cards, and a warning when the hook approaches.
- Animate the fish's startled escape and lunging pursuit, with growth after each smaller fish it catches and camera accents on upgrades.
- Synchronize dot, coin, hook, and feeding sounds with the action.
- Refresh the station montage and documentation.

## [v1.3] — 2026-11-15

- Rework the Cizhou scene: the fish pauses while dry-brush strokes, carved scales, weeds, and the final eye bring it to life, with a gentle camera push-in.
- Match the porcelain fish's glazed silhouette to its fins, refine the highlights, and soften its departure from the dish.
- Hold on the finished New Year print as the child smiles and blinks before the fish slips away.
- Extend the paper-cut sequence to show the outline and interior details being cut, with the scissors following each stage before the paper unfolds.
- Rebuild the code station as an editor: each completed drawing call restores one of the fish's earlier styles, then the render loop leads into the finale.
- Recompose the score around a shared ten-note theme, with different instruments and ornamentation at each station and notes timed to the code's drawing calls.
- Extend the film to 2:51.4, update chapter timings, and refresh the cover, station montage, and documentation.

## [v1.2] — 2026-11-10

- Keep the fish's color consistent as it settles onto the porcelain dish (1:17–1:18). It now takes on the dish's cobalt texture on contact, without overlapping fades.
- Let the code fish's probe sweep back and forth along the visible spine, appearing gradually as the fish enters the screen (2:14).
- Place live readings beside what they measure: heading and speed at the head, curvature at midbody, and wave displacement at the tail.

## [v1.1] — 2026-11-10

- Rework the oracle bone with a more organic outline, weathered surface, irregular cracks, and tapered knife cuts.
- Loosen the bronze fish's turn as it leaves the basin (0:46), and ease its slowdown onto the porcelain dish (1:17–1:18).
- Remove the vertical shadow seam between stations, most visible before the New Year print (1:40).
- Align the print transformation with the paper's edge. Keep the red frame behind the fish and stop frame borders and wall lines from showing through its body (1:41).
- Refine the paper-cut fish's scales, fins, and tail, keeping the silhouette connected through tight bends.
- Keep the two companion fish swimming in the Song painting during the finale, and remove the bright fin seam from the fish-shaped lighting (2:23).
- Add live motion readings and a moving probe to the code fish.
- Align the flute entrance with the clapper beat (0:54–0:55), and slightly raise the ambience, low drone, and keyboard sounds before the finale.
- Refresh the cover and station montage.

## [v1.0] — 2026-11-10

- Release the 2:34 film: eleven works of Chinese art, drawn in Canvas 2D with a synthesized Python score.
- Include the browser player, chapter navigation, frame stepping, still export, and parallel video rendering up to 4K at 60 fps.
